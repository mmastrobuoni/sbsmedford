Schools Beyond Screens is a national grassroots movement of parents and educators calling for school technology policies grounded in evidence.

Our Medford chapter includes families from all four elementary schools and both middle schools. We are not anti-tech; we are pro-learning.

We’re working with MPS leaders in a collaborative spirit to help create the best learning environment for every student.

Here’s what we’re advocating for:

01

Protect access for every student

We support devices and tools that help students with disabilities and language barriers communicate, access instruction, and participate in the classroom. We also support technology required by Individualized Education Programs (IEPs).

02

Our short-term goals

  1. Screen-free recess, lunch, and snack

    Keep individual devices and whole-class screens off during these times so students can talk, play, and connect.

  2. A choice about supplemental technology

    Allow parents and caregivers to opt their children out of technology that is not used for testing, when reasonable.

  3. Clear evidence of value and security

    Use apps and technology only when independent, peer-reviewed evidence supports their educational value and data security.

  4. Keep families informed

    Give parents and caregivers a clear picture of the apps their students use on school-issued devices.

  5. Active recess, outdoors whenever possible

    Make play and physical activity the focus of both indoor and outdoor recess. Help schools meet this goal by raising funds for recess supplies and equipment.

03

Our long-term goals

  1. Intentional screen limits by grade

    Set clear limits on devices and screen time, with more learning led by teachers and students.

  2. Limits on generative AI

    No student use of generative AI in kindergarten through grade 8, with limits for grades 9–12.

  3. Shared classroom devices

    Move away from a device for every student and return to a shared cart of devices in each classroom.

  4. Device-free school days

    Ban electronic devices, including cell phones and smart watches, from the first bell to the last in kindergarten through grade 12, with the access protections below.

  5. More active time beyond school

    Help adults and children disconnect from technology, get outdoors, and be active outside the school day.
